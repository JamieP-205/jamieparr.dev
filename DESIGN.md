# Design direction

Minimal page, technical detailing, in that order. Lots of white space and strong type do the work. Computing touches stay small and functional.

- **Type:** IBM Plex Sans for reading and IBM Plex Mono for labels, nav, dates and buttons. They're one family, so it reads as engineered, not decorated.
- **Colour:** warm off-white paper (#f6f5f1) and near-black ink. One accent, solder-mask green (#0f6b52), for actions. Dark copper (#945020) keeps dates and section labels readable in light mode. Dark mode uses neutral charcoal (#111316), with a muted sage accent (#9abbad). The header offers System, Light and Dark; System follows the device preference.
- **Circuit motifs:** a faint breadboard dot grid behind the hero, a thin vertical trace linking numbered section nodes (`01 // about`) on wide screens, and registration marks on the photo. No glowing circuits, no gradients, no icon grids.
- **Content lives in /content.** Words never go in templates.
